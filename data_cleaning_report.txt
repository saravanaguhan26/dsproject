=== Data Cleaning Report ===
Initial Dataset Shape: (101766, 50)

--- What was removed ---
Due to high missing values: ['weight']
Due to being identifiers: ['encounter_id', 'patient_nbr']
Due to constant values (useless): ['examide', 'citoglipton']
Due to possible data leakage: ['discharge_disposition_id', 'payer_code']
Duplicate rows removed: 0

--- What was retained ---
Retained 44 columns, including the original 'readmitted' column.
Note: max_glu_serum and A1Cresult were retained. The 'None' values in these columns signify 'Not Tested', which is clinically relevant and not an actual missing value.

--- How missing values were handled ---
1. '?' and standard NA strings were treated as missing values.
2. Features with >50% missing values (like weight) were dropped.
3. Remaining categorical features with missing values (e.g., race, medical_specialty, diag_1/2/3) were filled with the string 'Unknown'.
4. Any remaining numerical features with missing values were filled with their median (none expected).

--- How the target was created ---
New column 'readmission_risk' created based on 'readmitted':
 - '<30' was mapped to 1 (readmitted within 30 days)
 - '>30' was mapped to 0 (not readmitted within 30 days)
 - 'NO' was mapped to 0 (no readmission)

--- Class distribution of 'readmission_risk' ---
0 (No / >30 days): 90409 samples
1 (<30 days): 11357 samples

Final Dataset Shape: (101766, 44)
